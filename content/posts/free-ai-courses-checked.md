Search for "free AI course" and you get forty listicles that all copy each
other. Half the links are dead. One or two are paid and nobody mentions it.

So here is a shorter list. I opened every link before putting it here, and I
have written down what each one actually is, who made it, and what language it
is in.

The important part is not the number of links. It is that the first three are
made by the companies that built these tools, they are free, and almost nobody
knows they exist.

## Start with the official ones

The companies who built Claude and ChatGPT both launched their own free
courses. This is new. A year ago it did not exist.

**Claude Academy** — [academy.claude.com](https://academy.claude.com/)

Anthropic's own courses, expanded this month. The beginner track is *AI
Fluency: Framework and Foundations*, fourteen lessons and about four hours,
followed by *AI Capabilities and Limitations*, thirteen lessons. That second
one is the one to actually do. It teaches you what these tools cannot do,
which is the thing that stops you trusting a wrong answer.

If four hours sounds like a lot, there is a seven-minute tutorial called *The
4 Properties of AI*. Start there.

**OpenAI Academy** —
[applying AI at work](https://openai.com/index/academy-courses-applying-ai-at-work/)

OpenAI's own free courses, with free certificates at the end. Three of them:
AI Foundations, Applied AI Foundations, and Agents and Workflows.

**For Gemini**, Google has no equivalent free course for one person learning on
their own. The pages that look like one are aimed at schools and colleges
buying it for their staff. So open [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com),
sign in with the Google account you already have, and use the tutorials below.

## The paid one everybody calls free

**Google AI Essentials costs $49.** It is on Coursera, it is genuinely decent,
and it appears on nearly every "free AI courses" list you will find. It is not
free. If a list you are reading includes it without saying so, the person
writing it did not open the link.

## What to actually do today

Do not open thirteen tabs. You will close all of them.

Pick the tool you already use most. If that is ChatGPT, watch Charlie Chang's
tutorial. If it is Claude, do the seven-minute one on Claude Academy.

Then use it on one real task from your own work today. Not a practice question.
A real email, a real listing, a real assignment. That single step teaches you
more than three hours of watching.
