## Why it is shaped this way

- **Opens on an observation, not a definition.** "Most people think using AI
  means opening ChatGPT" is something the reader recognises about themselves.
- **The strongest single fact carries the post.** WhatsApp, not a list of five.
  A post that lists five things gets remembered as none of them.
- **The pricing caveat is the credibility.** Anyone can announce a free feature.
  Naming what is billable, with dates, is what makes the rest believable — and
  it will be the line practitioners reply to.
- **No emoji, no exclamation marks, no "unlock" or "game-changer".** The
  audience for this is people deciding whether you know what you are talking
  about.

## What to expect

Do not judge it on likes. The signal worth watching is whether anyone replies
saying they went and looked in their Tools tab. One of those is worth fifty
reactions, because it means the post produced an action rather than a scroll.

If it does well, the natural follow-up is not another list — it is what happened
when a real shop switched the agent on. That post cannot be written yet, which
is exactly why it would be worth writing later.
