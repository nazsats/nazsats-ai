Ready to copy and send. Every link points at the one live lesson:

`https://ai.nazsats.com/lessons/already-on-your-phone`

Four messages, spread over about two weeks. That is the whole campaign for this

---

## The rules these follow

Worth understanding before you edit them, because breaking these is what turns
a community into a broadcast channel people mute.

**Give the answer in the message. Put the detail behind the link.**
A message that says "5 amazing AI tools 🤯 click here" is spam and everyone
knows it. A message that tells you the actual thing, then offers the detail, is
a person sharing something. Same link, completely different reception.

**One idea per message.** Two makes both forgettable.

**At most two a week.** The fastest way to kill a 75-person group is to post in
it every day.

**Never two links in one message.** It halves the click on both.

## What NOT to do

- **Do not forward these to other groups yourself.** Let members do it. A
  message forwarded by a member reads as a recommendation; the same message
  forwarded by you reads as marketing.
- **Do not send a message with no value in it.** "New blog post is live 🔥" is
  the fastest way to get muted.
- **Do not follow up on silence.** If a message gets no reaction, the message
  was wrong, not the audience. Change the message.
- **Do not post the Blood Lab link in the group.** It is mentioned at the bottom
  of the lesson, which is the right amount. Pushing it in WhatsApp turns a
  community into a funnel and people feel the difference immediately.
