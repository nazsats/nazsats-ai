**Action:** I worked closely with the product team to understand data requirements, normalised tables to reduce redundancy, and wrote queries for reporting. I also reviewed slow queries and added indexes where needed.  
**Result:** This improved reporting performance and made it easier for staff to access the data they needed. I haven’t worked with PostgreSQL or managed databases at large scale yet, but I’m comfortable with SQL concepts and schema design, and I’m confident I can learn PostgreSQL specifics quickly.

IF THE INTERVIEW IS CONDUCTED BY AI  
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

- It is scored against a **rubric of job competencies**, not by counting keywords. Using lots of terms from the job ad without evidence is detected and counts against you.
- The AI cannot glance at your CV like a person. **Name your experience directly** — say “at Infobyte Solutions I designed MySQL schemas” rather than “as you can see in my CV.”
- **Structure matters more than usual**: Situation, Task, Action, Result, in that order, out loud.
- **Say the specific word when it’s honestly yours.** If you used Django, say “Django.” If you haven’t used Kubernetes, don’t say you have.
- **Don’t read a script.** Practise the shape of your answer, not the exact sentences.
- **If the software glitches** — audio drops, questions repeat, timer fails — email the recruiter and say so, factually. Ask to retake. This is reasonable and often granted.
