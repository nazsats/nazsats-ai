**IF THE INTERVIEW IS CONDUCTED BY AI**  
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

- It is scored against a **rubric of job competencies**, not by counting keywords. Using lots of terms from the job ad without substance will count against you.
- The AI cannot glance at your CV the way a person does. **Name the thing specifically** — for example, say “I built two landing pages in HTML and CSS for clients” rather than “I have some web experience.”
- **Structure carries more weight than usual**: clearly say Situation, Task, Action, Result, in that order, out loud.
- **Say the specific word when it is honestly yours.** If you have used a tool or system, name it. If not, do not mention it.
- **Reading a scripted answer off a screen is obvious** and will count against you. Practise the shape and flow of your answers, not memorised sentences.
- **If the software glitches** — audio drops, questions repeat, timer issues — email the recruiter afterwards and explain factually. Ask to retake. This is reasonable and often granted.
