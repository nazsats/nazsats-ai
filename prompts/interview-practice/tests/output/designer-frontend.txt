IF THE INTERVIEW IS CONDUCTED BY AI  
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

- It is scored against a **rubric of job competencies**, not by counting keywords. Don’t just repeat words from the job ad — focus on describing what you actually did, and how.
- The AI cannot skim your CV the way a person does. **Name the thing directly**: say “when I built two landing pages in HTML and CSS for clients” rather than “as you can see in my experience.”
- **Structure matters more than usual**: say Situation, Task, Action, Result, in that order, out loud.
- **Say the specific word when it’s honestly yours.** If you’ve used Figma, say “Figma.” If you haven’t used React, don’t claim it.
- **Don’t read a script word for word** — practice the shape and flow, not memorized sentences.
- **If the software glitches** (audio drops, repeated questions, timer issues), email the recruiter after and explain. Ask to retake if needed — this is common and reasonable.
