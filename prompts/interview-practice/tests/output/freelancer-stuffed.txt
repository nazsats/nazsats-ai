═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════  
IF THE INTERVIEW IS CONDUCTED BY AI  
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════  

- It is scored against a **rubric of job competencies**, not by counting keywords. Overusing terms from the job ad without substance can count against you.
- The AI cannot glance at your CV the way a person does. **Name the thing rather than gesturing at it** — say “at PixelCraft Studio I worked under an art director” rather than “as you can see in my CV”.
- **Structure carries more weight than usual** — say Situation, Task, Action, Result, in that order, out loud.
- **Say the specific word when it is honestly yours.** If you have used Figma, say “Figma”; if you have not used AWS, do not mention it.
- **Reading a scripted answer off a screen is obvious** and will lower your score. Practise the shape of your answer, not the exact sentences.
- **If the software glitches** — audio drops, questions repeat, timer fails — email the recruiter afterwards and say so, factually. Ask to sit it again. This is reasonable and often successful.

---

**Good luck!** If you want to practise answering any of these questions, just say so.
