═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════  
IF THE INTERVIEW IS CONDUCTED BY AI  
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

- Your answers are scored against a **rubric of job competencies** (like backend development, database design, testing, learning ability), not by keyword matching. Don’t just repeat words from the job ad.
- The AI cannot “see” your CV the way a person does. **Name your experience directly**: “At Infobyte Solutions, I built internal tools in Django,” not “as you can see from my experience.”
- **STAR structure is critical**: Situation, Task, Action, Result, in that order, out loud.
- **Say the specific word when it’s true**: If you used Django, say “Django.” If you haven’t used Docker, don’t mention it.
- **Don’t read a script**: Practise the shape of your answer, not the exact words.
- **If the software glitches** (audio drops, timer issues), email the recruiter factually and ask to retake. This is common and usually accepted.
