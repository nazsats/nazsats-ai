IF THE INTERVIEW IS CONDUCTED BY AI  
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

- Your answers are scored against a **rubric of job competencies** — not just keywords. Using lots of words from the job ad without substance is detected and can count against you.
- The AI **does not “see” your CV** the way a person does. Always **name the thing**: say “at Infobyte Solutions I built internal tools in Python and Django,” not “as you can see from my CV.”
- **Structure matters more than usual**. Say “Situation, Task, Action, Result” out loud, in order, so the rubric can score each part.
- If you have used a specific tool or method, **say its name** — “Django,” “MySQL,” etc. That’s vocabulary, not keyword stuffing.
- **Don’t read your answer word-for-word** — practice the shape, not the script.
- If the AI system glitches, **email the recruiter** and say so, factually. Ask to retake it. This is a normal and accepted thing to do.
