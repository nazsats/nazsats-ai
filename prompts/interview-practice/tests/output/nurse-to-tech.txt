IF THE INTERVIEW IS CONDUCTED BY AI  
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════  

- It is scored against a **rubric of job competencies**, not by counting keywords. Don’t just repeat words from the job ad; focus on giving clear, structured answers.
- The AI cannot glance at your CV the way a person does. **Name the thing specifically** — for example, say “I used Epic EHR for patient records” rather than “I used hospital software.”
- **Structure matters even more** — say Situation, Task, Action, Result, in that order, out loud.
- **Say the specific word when it’s honestly yours.** If you have used Epic EHR, say “Epic EHR.” Don’t call it “an electronic system” if you can be specific.
- **Don’t read answers off a screen.** Practise the shape and flow of your answers so you sound natural.
- **If the software glitches** — audio drops, questions repeat, timer acts up — email the recruiter, explain what happened, and ask to retake. This is common and is not held against you when reported.
