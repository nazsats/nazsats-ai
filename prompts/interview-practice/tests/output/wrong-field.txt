IF THE INTERVIEW IS CONDUCTED BY AI  
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════  

- It is scored against a **rubric of job competencies**, not by counting keywords. Using lots of terms from the job ad without evidence of experience is detected and counts against you.
- The AI cannot glance at your CV the way a person does. **Name the thing rather than gesturing at it** — for example, say "at Lilavati Hospital I managed patient documentation in Epic," not "as you can see from my experience."
- **Structure carries more weight than usual** — Situation, Task, Action, Result, in that order, out loud.
- **Say the specific word when it is honestly yours.** If you have used Epic, say "Epic." If you have not used Python, do not say "Python."
- **Reading a scripted answer off a screen is obvious** and will count against you. Practise the shape and flow of your answer, not the exact sentences.
- **If the software glitches** — audio drops, a question repeats, or the timer malfunctions — email the recruiter afterwards and explain what happened. Ask to retake the interview. This is reasonable and often granted, and reporting a real fault is evidence of professionalism.
